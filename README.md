## WordPress developer and automation builder

I am a Florida-based web and IT specialist working remotely in English and Spanish. I have worked in web and IT for more than 15 years and have built and maintained WordPress sites since 2019.

I like practical work: a faster site, a clean WordPress build, or an automation that removes repetitive admin work.

- Site performance, security, SSL, DNS, hosting, and migrations
- Business workflows connected with APIs, webhooks, Zapier, Make, and n8n
- Focused websites for service businesses and digital products
